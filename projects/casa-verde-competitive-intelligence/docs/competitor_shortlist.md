## Criterion checks and evidence limits

### Urbane Design

- **Residential and geography, firm claim:** Misty Hill is identified as an Austin residential remodel completed in 2019, involving a bathroom and laundry reconfiguration. [Misty Hill project](https://urbanedesignstudios.com/project/misty-hill-remodel/)
- **Sustainability and honest materials, firm claim:** The homepage describes durability and reuse of antique pieces. The named project specifies reclaimed-wood sliding doors. This supports a concrete reuse example, not a measured environmental outcome. [Homepage](https://urbanedesignstudios.com/), [Misty Hill](https://urbanedesignstudios.com/project/misty-hill-remodel/)
- **Budget, unknown:** No comparable numerical total-project range was found on the reviewed homepage, services, or Misty Hill page. Services cover remodeling, construction, and furnishing. Next check: detailed service pages and any public investment guidance during Phase 2b. [Services](https://urbanedesignstudios.com/services/)
- **Instagram identity, observed:** The live services page links to `@theurbanedesign`. This confirms the intended account link, not access to its grid. [Official linking page](https://urbanedesignstudios.com/services/)

### Stelly Selway

- **Residential and geography, firm claim:** Rosedale is a 1940s bungalow renovation in Austin's Rosedale neighborhood. Its completion date is not stated in the reviewed text; media and award dates are not completion dates. [Rosedale](https://www.stellyselway.com/project/rosedale)
- **Sustainability and honest materials, firm claim:** The studio describes durable work and vintage sourcing. Rosedale names reclaimed pine, mahogany, blackened oak, and ash. Only the pine is explicitly identified as reclaimed in that project description. [Studio approach](https://www.stellyselway.com/), [Rosedale](https://www.stellyselway.com/project/rosedale)
- **Budget, unknown:** No numerical project range was found on the reviewed studio, Rosedale, or contact pages. Next check: public service or investment information for Austin residential work, separate from international work. [Contact page](https://www.stellyselway.com/contact)
- **Instagram identity, observed:** The live Rosedale page footer links to `@stellyselway`. [Official linking page](https://www.stellyselway.com/project/rosedale)

### Urbanspace Design Studio

- **Residential and geography, firm claim:** East Residence is labeled residential, Austin, and completed in 2024. The studio also serves commercial and larger development projects, so the comparison must isolate residential interiors. [East Residence](https://urbanspacedesign.com/project/eastresidence/), [About](https://urbanspacedesign.com/about/)
- **Sustainability and honest materials, firm claim:** The project description states a sustainable-design priority and names a handle made from reclaimed wood. Do not describe the entire door as reclaimed. The kitchen description also distinguishes existing and new panels. [East Residence](https://urbanspacedesign.com/project/eastresidence/)
- **Budget, observed with unknown basis:** The live contact form presents USD $50,000 to $100,000, $100,000 to $200,000, $200,000 to $300,000, and $300,000+ inquiry choices. These are intake categories, not quotes, accepted minimums, or completed project costs. Whether they include design fees, furniture, construction, taxes, or all of these is **unknown**. Next check: published scope and fee guidance. No form was submitted. [Contact form](https://urbanspacedesign.com/contact/)
- **Instagram identity, observed:** The live site's navigation links to `@urbanspacedesignstudio`. Use this account for the studio rather than conflating it with its related retail or real estate accounts. [Official linking page](https://urbanspacedesign.com/contact/)

### J.Fisher Interiors

- **Residential and geography, firm claim:** The Hyde Park project describes a 2,000-square-foot condo renovation, including its kitchen and 2.5 bathrooms. The service page explicitly offers Austin residential work. [Hyde Park](https://jfisherinteriors.com/portfolio/hyde-park/), [Residential service page](https://jfisherinteriors.com/services/residential-interior-design/architectural-interior-designers/)
- **Sustainability and honest materials, firm claim:** The service page specifies reclaimed wood, low-VOC finishes, and efficient lighting as practices. Hyde Park names white oak cabinetry, stone, terracotta, and soapstone, but does not establish that its wood is reclaimed or verify its finish emissions. Keep the project facts separate from the general claims. [Service claims](https://jfisherinteriors.com/services/residential-interior-design/architectural-interior-designers/), [Project materials](https://jfisherinteriors.com/portfolio/hyde-park/)
- **Budget, unknown:** No numerical project price was found on the reviewed service or project pages. The introductory design guide is behind a name-and-email form; its contents were not accessed. Next check: other publicly accessible service or fee guidance. [Guide landing page](https://jfisherinteriors.com/the-interior-design-process-demystified/)
- **Instagram identity, observed:** The live service and project pages link to `@jfisherinteriors`. [Official linking page](https://jfisherinteriors.com/portfolio/hyde-park/)

### Studio Ferme

- **Residential and geography, firm claim:** The 1,024-square-foot 7th Street Cottage is in Austin's Castle Hill neighborhood, with a renovation described as completed in 2025. [7th Street Cottage](https://www.studioferme.com/7th-street-cottage)
- **Sustainability and honest materials, firm claim:** The project retained its footprint and reused original shiplap. The About page explicitly discusses sourcing, material life cycles, and reuse. This makes it a useful thematic benchmark without independently proving its environmental results. [Cottage](https://www.studioferme.com/7th-street-cottage), [About](https://www.studioferme.com/about)
- **Budget, unknown for comparable scope:** The reviewed About and cottage pages do not provide a numerical fee or project range. The Theresa Passive House page links to a media headline reporting USD $836,000 for that renovation. That is outside Casa Verde's stated band, but its detailed cost basis has not been verified here and it does not establish the studio's minimum or full range. Next check: scope-specific pricing and the linked cost breakdown if publicly accessible. [Theresa Passive House](https://www.studioferme.com/theresa-passive-house)
- **Availability and Instagram identity, observed:** The live About page states a sabbatical and discusses possible future projects; no return date was found. Its navigation links to `@studioferme`. Do not characterize it as an immediately available alternative. [About](https://www.studioferme.com/about)
