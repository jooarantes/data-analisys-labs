# Urbanspace Design Studio

Research date and access date for every source: **2026-09-16**. Phase 2b. Inclusion: **provisional fit; comparable budget unverified**.

Evidence was inspected in the connected live browser using Playwright and accessibility views. Status labels distinguish observed public content, firm claims, research inferences, and unknowns. Except for the dates stated below, source publication dates are unknown. Raw screenshots and snapshots preserve original wording; authored text uses faithful paraphrases.

## 1. Positioning sentence
**Firm claim, paraphrase of firm positioning:** An Austin design studio working across homes, hospitality, workplaces, and larger developments, with interiors shaped around how people use a space. Sustainability is explicit in East Residence, but is not the central promise of the reviewed homepage or Instagram bio. [Homepage][home], [About][about], [East Residence][east], [Instagram][ig].

## 2. Project types served
**Firm claim:** Full-service interiors, custom furniture, and construction oversight across residential and commercial work. **Observed sample:** five entries from the residential portfolio: The Hills, Clarksville Home, Modern Penthouse, East Residence, and Avon Retreat. East Residence describes a condo renovation with existing-panel matching, new surfaces, and a room-dividing system. The other entries mainly provide galleries; Avon has residential, location, and year metadata. Scope must not be invented from their titles. [About][about], [Portfolio][portfolio], [The Hills][hills], [Clarksville Home][clark], [Modern Penthouse][pent], [East Residence][east], [Avon Retreat][avon].

East Residence and Avon are dated 2024. Dates and scope for the other three are **unknown** in the reviewed text. The gallery does not prove a latest-five order. This sample supports a residential interiors alternative, while the larger commercial offer is kept separate.

## 3. Signature materials
**Firm claim, one project:** East Residence names Taj Mahal stone, new kitchen panels matched to existing ones, a nine-foot handle made of reclaimed wood, a scalloped wood wall, and a Rimadesio sliding system. The **handle**, not the entire door, is identified as reclaimed. Its prior use and wood species are **unknown**. [East Residence][east].

No named material is documented across two sampled projects, so a recurring signature is **unknown**, insufficient written sample. No native species or product-level emissions documents were found in these cases. Next check: annotated schedules for at least one additional residential project. [The Hills][hills], [Clarksville Home][clark], [Modern Penthouse][pent], [Avon Retreat][avon].

## 4. Pricing range
**Observed intake choices, unknown cost basis:** The contact form offers USD 50,000 to 100,000; 100,000 to 200,000; 200,000 to 300,000; and 300,000+. These are inquiry categories, not a quote, project minimum, or accepted price range. Inclusions such as design fees, construction, furniture, taxes, and procurement are **unknown**. No form was submitted. [Contact][contact].

Total project range, hourly rate, retainer, design fee, and procurement charges remain **unknown, not disclosed** in the reviewed sources. Comparable budget fit is **unverified**, despite numerical overlap in intake categories. East Residence is a documented condo renovation; a reliable smallest-to-largest ranking is **unknown** because the other four cases lack sufficiently comparable scope descriptions. Next check: explicit investment guidance and project scope summaries. [East Residence][east], [Portfolio][portfolio].

## 5. Geographic focus in Austin
**Firm claim and observed metadata:** Austin is the studio base and East Residence is explicitly located there. Avon Retreat identifies Avon, Colorado. The remaining three sampled project locations are **unknown** from reviewed text; a neighborhood-like project title alone is not sufficient confirmation. The sample is one confirmed Austin location, one Colorado location, and three unresolved locations. [About][about], [East Residence][east], [Avon Retreat][avon], [The Hills][hills], [Clarksville Home][clark], [Modern Penthouse][pent].

## 7. Ideal client persona
**Inference, moderate confidence:** A homeowner planning a detailed condo interior who wants furniture and architectural selections coordinated by a studio also experienced in larger, mixed-use work. East Residence and the stated end-to-end service support this interpretation. Client income, developer status, and budget are not inferred. [East Residence][east], [About][about].

## 10. Weakness or gap
**Communication gap, moderate confidence:** East Residence provides a specific reclaimed detail, but comparable explanation is missing from the other four sampled cases. A sustainability-focused homeowner cannot determine whether that practice recurs. The studio's commercial breadth is a strategic choice, not itself a weakness. If Casa Verde can document its own work consistently, a residential case library with retained elements and supplier records would make comparison easier. [East Residence][east], [The Hills][hills], [Clarksville Home][clark], [Modern Penthouse][pent], [Avon Retreat][avon].
