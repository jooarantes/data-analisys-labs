# Urbane Design

Research date and access date for every source: **2026-09-16**. Phase 2b. Inclusion: **provisional fit; comparable budget unverified**.

Evidence was inspected in the connected live browser using Playwright and accessibility views. Status labels distinguish observed public content, firm claims, research inferences, and unknowns. Except for the dates stated below, source publication dates are unknown. Raw screenshots and snapshots preserve original wording; authored text uses faithful paraphrases.

## 1. Positioning sentence
**Firm claim, paraphrase of firm positioning:** An Austin full-service interiors practice helping clients express their individuality through new construction, remodeling, and furniture selection. Sustainability appears as durability and antique reuse within that broader offer, rather than the main headline. The Instagram bio emphasizes services and recognition. [Homepage][home], [About][about], [Instagram][ig].

## 2. Project types served
**Firm claim:** Remodeling, new construction, furnishing, and virtual design are advertised; remodeling can include planning, finish selection, contractor coordination, and smaller consultations. [Services][services], [Remodeling][remodel].

**Observed portfolio sample: five distinct residential projects.** Misty Hill is a 2019 bathroom and laundry reconfiguration; Big View covers primary and guest bathrooms in 2020. Barton Creek is labeled new construction in 2023; 360 Condo and Allium Drive are labeled remodels in 2023. The latter three provide metadata and galleries but little written decision context. These are credible scope substitutes for Casa Verde, with budget fit unverified. [Misty Hill][misty], [Big View][big], [Barton Creek][barton], [360 Condo][condo], [Allium Drive][allium].

The gallery is not reliably sorted by completion date. The sample combines three dated 2023 examples with two older cases containing material explanations; it is not a verified latest-five sample. No completion dates were inferred from image filenames.

## 3. Signature materials
**Firm claims tied to individual projects:** Misty Hill specifies reclaimed-wood sliding doors for the laundry and water closet. Species, previous use, and supplier are **unknown**. Big View names naturally stained white oak cabinetry, concrete-finish walls, handmade tile purchased through Clay Imports, Brazilian black slate, porcelain, and quartz. The Austin supplier location does not establish local manufacture. [Misty Hill][misty], [Big View][big].

No named material meets the two-project recurrence rule in this bounded sample. Native species, finish emissions, and care instructions are **unknown**, not inferable from room photographs. Next check: project specifications or a published sourcing schedule.

## 4. Pricing range
**unknown, not disclosed:** Total residential project range, design fees, hourly rate, retainer, procurement charges, and minimum engagement were not established on the reviewed services, remodeling, and five project pages. Award budget categories on the homepage are not a studio price list. Budget fit with Casa Verde's USD 40,000 to 300,000 band remains **unverified**. [Services][services], [Remodeling][remodel], [Homepage][home].

Smallest demonstrated scope in this sample: bathroom and laundry work at Misty Hill. Broadest: Barton Creek new construction, with area and cost **unknown**. These are scope examples, not cost endpoints. Next check: public investment guidance with inclusions separated from construction and furnishing costs. [Misty Hill][misty], [Barton Creek][barton].

## 5. Geographic focus in Austin
**Firm claim, supported by project labels:** Four of five sampled projects identify Austin, including Northwest Austin for Misty Hill and Big View. Barton Creek identifies Dripping Springs. This documents both Austin and nearby Hill Country relevance; it does not establish neighborhood dominance. The office is in Austin. [Misty Hill][misty], [Big View][big], [360 Condo][condo], [Allium Drive][allium], [Barton Creek][barton], [About][about].

## 7. Ideal client persona
**Inference, moderate confidence:** Austin-area homeowners who want a designer to guide a room renovation or new-build interior while making space for existing character and individual tastes. Service breadth and Misty Hill's practical layout changes support this reading. No income, age, or willingness to pay is inferred. [Services][services], [Misty Hill][misty].

## 10. Weakness or gap
**Communication and evidence gap, moderate confidence:** The strong reclaimed-door example is from 2019, while the three sampled 2023 pages do not explain retention or sourcing choices. This makes continuity harder for a client to assess. The August 2026 kitchen-retention preview is counterevidence, but does not close the specification gap. If Casa Verde can substantiate its work, it could publish the retained element, origin, reason for keeping it, and care plan in every new case study. This is a documentation opportunity, not a finding of poor environmental practice. [Misty Hill][misty], [Barton Creek][barton], [360 Condo][condo], [Allium Drive][allium], [recent retention preview](https://www.instagram.com/theurbanedesign/p/DcJwvAZGoB7/).
