## 1. Positioning sentence
**Firm claim, paraphrase of firm positioning:** An Austin residential interior design studio integrating material life cycles, climate resilience, and everyday comfort through a mindful approach to homes. Instagram's bio echoes nature and resilience. **Availability caveat:** the live About page states that the studio is on sabbatical and discusses possible future work; a return date and current intake are **unknown**. [About][about], [Instagram][ig].

## 2. Project types served
**Firm claim:** New construction, renovations and additions, custom millwork and furniture, decoration, and styling. The studio also mentions consulting for housing, education, and nonprofit spaces. The future-work note favors major renovation or new construction with aligned architects and a full furnishing scope; do not assume that all earlier small engagements remain available. [About][about].

**Observed residential sample:** all three residential-oriented entries identifiable in the portfolio: Theresa Passive House, 7th Street Cottage, and Maude Street Millwork. Theresa is a 2,100-square-foot renovation and addition completed in 2020. The cottage is a 1,024-square-foot 1924 bungalow renovated in 2025, keeping the footprint while adding a second full bathroom. Maude is a millwork design commission, with completion date and residential context less fully described. Cedar Sukkah, travel, and food imagery were not used to inflate the residential sample. [Portfolio][home], [Theresa][theresa], [Cottage][cottage], [Maude Street][maude].

## 3. Signature materials
**Firm claim:** The cottage reuses painted shiplap from the original home. This supplies actual prior-use provenance, with species and finish chemistry **unknown**. The About page discusses local sourcing, low-toxicity choices, and material life cycles, but these are practice statements rather than product evidence. Native-plant species and care specifications are **unknown**. Next check: material schedules for the two documented renovations. [About][about].

## 10. Weakness or gap
**Availability uncertainty, high confidence in the notice and limited confidence about future intake:** The studio states a sabbatical without a return date in the reviewed About page. A client starting now cannot establish availability from that page alone. The stated future-project conditions are helpful counterevidence against calling the practice closed. If Casa Verde has verified capacity, clear start windows and a defined engagement process could differentiate its offer. Material provenance alone is not an uncontested opening here: the cottage and externally documented Theresa project already provide substantive evidence.
