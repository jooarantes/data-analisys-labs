# Casa Verde positioning deck summary

Research cutoff: 2026-09-16. Website recaptures: 2026-09-17.

| Slide | Title | Key takeaway |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Austin sustainable interiors | Casa Verde's clearest opening is a consistent project record around provenance, retention, care, and scope. |
| 2 | Austin's proof gap | Reuse appears across the field, while comparable pricing and repeatable material records remain difficult to verify. |
| 3 | Five firms, five different kinds of proof | Each firm brings a different mix of residential scope, material evidence, and public clarity. |
| 4 | Urbane Design | Urbane shows broad remodeling capability, with reclaimed material evidence concentrated in isolated cases. |
| 5 | Stelly Selway | Stelly links maker relationships and reuse to several projects, while previous-use and care records vary. |
| 6 | Urbanspace Design Studio | East Residence supplies a detailed example, but recurrence across the residential portfolio remains unknown. |
| 7 | J.Fisher Interiors | J.Fisher shows recurring white oak and soapstone, while broad low-VOC claims lack product-level records. |
| 8 | Studio Ferme | Studio Ferme has substantive preservation evidence and outside corroboration for one project, with current availability unresolved. |
| 9 | Positioning map | Public evidence depth and demonstrated residential scope separate the five firms more clearly than visual style alone. |
| 10 | Spaces Casa Verde can test and own | Material ledgers, investment clarity, native planting records, and visible capacity form four testable openings. |
| 11 | The next 30 days | The team can turn the positioning hypothesis into proof through a project audit, a shared case-study format, clearer investment examples, and client testing. |
| 12 | Casa Verde can make the material story easier to verify | Casa Verde's strongest story connects honest materials to a record clients can understand and trust. |

The deck uses only the 2026-09-17 website recaptures for competitor imagery. Pricing remains unverified across all five firms, and the positioning map measures public evidence rather than environmental performance or service quality.

Typography note: GT Sectra and Söhne were unavailable in the local font set. The deck uses Georgia for headings and Aptos for body copy.
