# Website recapture plan

Visit date: 2026-09-17. The existing research remains dated 2026-09-16.

## Scope and audit

Visual review of all 47 original website PNGs identified 46 unique source URLs across the five existing firms. The two AIA captures belong to one URL. Instagram and competitor selection are outside this round. Original files will remain intact.

The audit used contact sheets of every original website PNG. A partial view can be useful evidence but does not establish full page coverage. Mid-Atlantic is the first recapture: its original shows the introduction and omits the room galleries.

## Capture method

Use the connected Playwright browser at 1440 × 1000 CSS pixels. Scroll progressively to the bottom, wait for visible photographs and transitions, and capture the complete document. Inspect interactive galleries separately. For project pages, add two to four distinct room or gallery views where available. For long institutional pages, add readable section views. Capture visible page elements without changing their wording or styling. Record remaining loading failures and unavailable states.

Save PNGs in `assets/screenshots/websites/<slug>/recapture-2026-09-17/`. Each capture must have a source URL, actual visit date, browser and image dimensions, section or gallery state, original counterpart, limitations, and visual review result. Review images before accepting them. Missing evidence remains `unknown`. Photographs do not establish material provenance or environmental performance.
