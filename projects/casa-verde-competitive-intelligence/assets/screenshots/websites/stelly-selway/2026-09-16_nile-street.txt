- generic: Interior Renovation in Austin, Texas
- paragraph: A thoughtful renovation in Austin, Texas for private clients, designed as a calm and materially expressive urban home. Stelly Selway reworked the kitchen and dining space as the centre of the home, balancing functionality with a refined, layered aesthetic. Lighting plays a defining role, with sculptural fixtures by Volker Haug, WDSTCK and Thorup Copenhagen establishing atmosphere and rhythm across the space.
- paragraph: A rich material palette of oak, black soapstone and aged brass creates contrast and depth, with bespoke joinery and moss-green accents softening the architectural lines. Custom banquette seating frames the dining area, while integrated artwork introduces a more personal and curated dimension. The result is a highly considered interior where light, material and form work together to create a quietly expressive and enduring space.
