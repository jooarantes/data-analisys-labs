# Casa Verde Studio, Brand Bible

## Company Snapshot
Casa Verde Studio is a sustainable interior design firm based in Austin, Texas, founded in 2022 by Elena Reyes. Specializes in residential and small commercial projects that use reclaimed wood, native plants, low-VOC materials, and locally sourced furniture.

## Founder
- **Elena Reyes**, 41, formerly senior designer at a corporate architecture firm in Houston
- Lives in Hyde Park, Austin
- Believes a home should feel like it can breathe
- Hates fast furniture and beige open-plan everything

## Studio
- One studio off East 6th Street in Austin
- Team of four: Elena plus three designers and one project coordinator

## Mission
Design homes and small spaces that feel calm, alive, and built to last more than five years.

## Voice and Tone
- Warm, calm, plant-loving without being precious
- Anti-corporate, soft confident
- Sensory language: light, breath, texture, time
- Never aggressive or salesy

## Sample Voice
**Yes:**
> "We pulled the sycamore from a Brooklyn workshop that salvages from local renovations. Each board still has the original grain. The room remembers where the wood came from."

**No:**
> "Transform your space with our luxury, premium, designer-curated furniture solutions!"

## Signature Phrase
> "Design that breathes."

Use it as a sign-off, a tagline, and a guiding principle. Never overuse it on the same page.

## Vocabulary Preferences
**Use these:**
- "live-in," "lived-in," "honest materials," "patina," "grain," "weight," "porous," "breathe," "light reads better when..."
- "we sourced from," "we pulled from," "we left the original"

**Avoid these:**
- "luxury," "premium," "elevated," "curated," "bespoke"
- "transform," "stunning," "gorgeous," "wow factor"
- Adjective stacking like "beautifully stunning gorgeous space"

## Materials Library (real preferences)
- **Wood:** reclaimed oak, sycamore, white oak, cedar
- **Stone:** limestone, travertine, soapstone (no high-polish granite)
- **Textiles:** linen, raw cotton, jute, undyed wool
- **Plants:** monstera, fiddle leaf, native Texas grasses, olive trees indoor
- **Paint:** Farrow & Ball, Backdrop, all low-VOC

## Customer Persona
- Homeowners 35 to 60 who care about provenance
- Small business owners renovating offices that feel like homes
- Architecture appreciators who hate formula
- Clients with budgets between $40k and $300k for a project

## Do's
- Talk about where materials came from
- Mention specific suppliers and craftspeople
- Use one strong sensory detail per paragraph
- Sign off Instagram captions with "Design that breathes."

## Don'ts
- No before-and-after dramatic music reels
- No "transform your space" hooks
- No exclamation marks
- No stock-photo-feeling Instagram posts
- No discount language

## Reusable Phrases
- "Design that breathes."
- "Materials with a memory."
- "Built for the next twenty years, not the next trend."
- "We pulled from..."
- "The room remembers."
