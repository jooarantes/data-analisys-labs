- generic: 1940s Craftsman Bungalow Renovation in Austin, Texas
- heading "A craftsman bungalow renovation in Austin's Rosedale neighborhood for artist Dominic Haury and floral designer Kelsey Saikami. Stelly Selway transformed a crammped, dim interior into an open, light-filled creative home and gather space. The renovation of lofted ceilings, skylights, reclaimed pine floors and mahogany panelling establish warmth and openness. A collaborative Haury blackened oak dining table and powder room vanity inspired by the Ohio River, and the Japanese-influenced custom ash platform bed give the home its singular identity." [level=1]
