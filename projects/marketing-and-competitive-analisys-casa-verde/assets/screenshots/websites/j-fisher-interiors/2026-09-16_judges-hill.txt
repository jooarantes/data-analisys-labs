- heading "Judges Hill" [level=1]
- paragraph: This 5,500-square-foot Judges Hill residence was remodeled in close collaboration with a longtime friend who lovingly refers to me as her “housewife.” The project carried the ease and joy of working with family, making the process as meaningful as the finished design. Together, we set out to refresh a mid-century home that had seen several renovations over the years, with the goal of preserving its character while layering in new personality, color, and timeless details.
- paragraph: Respecting the home’s bones, we made selective changes that had a big impact. The primary suite was elevated with new stone finishes, while outdated features, including a urinal in the bathroom, were replaced with elements that felt more aligned with the homeowner’s style. The back porch was reimagined for better connection to the interior, creating a stronger flow between indoor and outdoor living. Many existing pieces were reused and reworked, paired with new furnishings, finishes, and custom touches to add dimension and individuality without losing the spirit of what was already there.
- paragraph: The result is a home that blends mid-century charm with updated elegance, infused with the humor, warmth, and creativity of a true collaboration between friends. Judges Hill reflects not only thoughtful design but also the trust and joy that come from working with someone who feels like family.
