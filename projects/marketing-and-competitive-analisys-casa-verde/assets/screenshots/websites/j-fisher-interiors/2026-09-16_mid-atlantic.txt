- heading "Mid Atlantic" [level=1]
- paragraph: An empty 6,000-square-foot residence became a jewel-toned retreat defined by bold color, custom design, and richly layered interiors. Working with a repeat client, the goal was to transform a blank property into a home full of warmth and character. With no budget limitations, the design embraced the use of bespoke furnishings, custom rugs, and unique furniture pieces to ensure that every space felt both distinctive and deeply personal.
- paragraph: Each room was infused with vibrant color and expressive textures, accented by statement lighting and carefully chosen accessories. The effect is a home that is lively and engaging yet sophisticated and comfortable. Designed as both a retreat and a reflection of the client’s appreciation for unique design, the Mid-Atlantic residence is a striking example of luxury interiors brought to life through personality and detail.
