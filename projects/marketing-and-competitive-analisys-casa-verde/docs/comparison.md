# Austin sustainable residential interiors: comparison

Evidence cutoff: **2026-09-16**. Consolidated: **2026-09-17**. This is Phase 2b of Casa Verde's research build, using the [locked framework](research_framework.md) and the five firms retained after the [shortlist checkpoint](competitor_shortlist.md).

The clearest distinction in this sample is how consistently firms connect material claims to named projects. Reuse already appears in several practices, so Casa Verde cannot credibly treat reclaimed materials as an unoccupied position. A more useful hypothesis is to make provenance, finish choices, care, and engagement scope easier to verify. That remains a hypothesis about communication, not proof of unmet demand.

**Selection limits:** 0 confirmed budget fits and 5 provisional fits. Comparable total-project pricing remains unknown for every firm. Studio Ferme discloses a sabbatical and is retained as a benchmark, not an assumed immediately available competitor. Five purposively selected firms cannot establish citywide prevalence, market share, or demand. The brand bible supplies Casa Verde's stated USD 40,000 to 300,000 band and material priorities, not independently verified results. [Shortlist](competitor_shortlist.md), [Studio Ferme availability](https://www.studioferme.com/about), [brand bible](../../brand/casa_verde_brand_bible.md).

## Positioning hypotheses for Elena's team

1. **Make proof routine.** Reuse is already documented by several firms. If Casa Verde has the records, publish a short material ledger for each project: what remained, where additions came from, finish specification, and care. The opening is consistency of explanation, not a claim to have introduced reuse. [Stelly Selway](competitors/stelly-selway.md#6-sustainability-claims), [Studio Ferme](competitors/studio-ferme.md#6-sustainability-claims).
2. **Explain investment with scope.** A clear description of what Casa Verde's stated band includes could help clients self-select. This is an information-design hypothesis, not evidence that Casa Verde is cheaper. No comparable five-firm price ranking is supported. [Pricing comparison](#side-by-side-comparison), [brand bible](../../brand/casa_verde_brand_bible.md).
3. **Connect material care and native planting to actual choices.** Named regional plant suitability and consistent care guidance were not established in the reviewed profiles. If Casa Verde can document those practices, test whether clients value them. The sample does not prove competitors lack such expertise. [Material and evidence fields](#side-by-side-comparison).
4. **Show capacity and the working process.** A verified start window and clear responsibilities could reduce uncertainty for clients. Studio Ferme's sabbatical is a specific example, not evidence of a wider supply shortage. [Availability evidence](competitors/studio-ferme.md#10-weakness-or-gap).

## Access limits and handoff

All five websites were reached in the live browser. Some pages loaded slowly or first produced incomplete captures; targeted retries recovered the text and key images. Instagram grids were public, but Urbane and J.Fisher reached signup walls before complete historical coverage. Individual caption previews were often truncated. No login, outreach, or form submission was performed. The [capture log](capture_log.md) distinguishes successful pages, partial evidence, and retained limitations.

Project dates were not consistently published, so the website sample cannot be represented as the five most recent projects for every firm. Rates use the frozen 90-day window only where its public-feed boundary could be inspected. No full-caption quality ranking is supported. Preserve these limitations, the provisional status, and the 2026-09-16 cutoff in prompts 3 and 4. No deck, public paper, or social publication has been produced in this phase.
