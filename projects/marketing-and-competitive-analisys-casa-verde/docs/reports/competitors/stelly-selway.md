# Stelly Selway

Research date and access date for every source: **2026-09-16**. Phase 2b. Inclusion: **provisional fit; comparable budget unverified**.

Evidence was inspected in the connected live browser using Playwright and accessibility views. Status labels distinguish observed public content, firm claims, research inferences, and unknowns. Except for the dates stated below, source publication dates are unknown. Raw screenshots and snapshots preserve original wording; authored text uses faithful paraphrases.

## 1. Positioning sentence
**Firm claim, paraphrase of firm positioning:** An Austin and London interior design studio creating personal spaces through durable materials, vintage pieces, and relationships with makers. The website includes private residential, developer, and hospitality work; the Instagram bio leads with locations and editorial recognition. [Homepage][home], [About][about], [Instagram][ig].

## 2. Project types served
**Firm claim:** Residential interiors and renovation alongside developer and hospitality commissions. **Observed sample:** five residential projects: Rosedale, Nile Street, and Lafayette Avenue in Austin; Whitehall Road and Upper Mall in London. Rosedale and Lafayette involve 1940s bungalows. Nile Street is a kitchen and dining renovation. Upper Mall includes a basement, loft, rear addition, kitchen, four bathrooms, four reception rooms, and five bedrooms. The international project demonstrates scope, not an Austin budget. [About][about], [Rosedale][rose], [Nile Street][nile], [Lafayette Avenue][laf], [Whitehall Road][white], [Upper Mall][upper].

Completion dates are **unknown** on the reviewed project narratives. Press and award years were not substituted for completion dates. The gallery does not establish a reliable latest-five order, so this is a bounded sample including three local cases.

## 3. Signature materials
**Firm claim:** Rosedale specifies reclaimed pine flooring, mahogany paneling, a blackened-oak table and vanity made with Dominic Haury, and a custom ash bed. Nile Street names oak, black soapstone, and aged brass. Oak is recurring across two distinct projects, but species and reclaimed status are not the same claim: Rosedale's reclaimed wood is pine. [Rosedale][rose], [Nile Street][nile].

Lafayette Avenue describes reclaimed materials and vintage furnishings without a full sourcing schedule. Whitehall Road names Bauwerk limewash, original millwork, raffia, and flax. Upper Mall names timber, hemp, linen, and custom stone vanities. Geographic origin, emissions documentation, and maintenance requirements remain **unknown** for most selections. [Lafayette Avenue][laf], [Whitehall Road][white], [Upper Mall][upper]. A 2026-08-04 social preview explicitly identifies dead-stock military canvas for a bath curtain, adding a recent reuse signal outside the five-case website sample. [Post](https://www.instagram.com/stellyselway/p/DbnTME4E6Cl/).

## 4. Pricing range
**unknown, not disclosed:** Total project range, design fees, hourly rates, retainers, procurement charges, and minimum engagement were not found on About, Contact, or the five project pages. Budget fit remains **unverified**. [About][about], [Contact][contact], [Projects][portfolio].

Smallest demonstrated engagement in the sample: Nile Street kitchen and dining renovation. Broadest: Upper Mall's multi-level renovation and additions in London. Neither supplies a cost. Next check: published Austin residential investment guidance with construction, furnishing, and design components separated. [Nile Street][nile], [Upper Mall][upper].

## 5. Geographic focus in Austin
**Observed source coverage:** Three of five sampled cases identify Austin: Rosedale, Cherrywood for Lafayette Avenue, and Nile Street. Two identify London. Austin and London are both stated bases. The sample supports local bungalow work, not exclusive Austin specialization or a quantified local market share. [About][about], [Rosedale][rose], [Lafayette Avenue][laf], [Nile Street][nile], [Whitehall Road][white], [Upper Mall][upper].

## 7. Ideal client persona
**Inference, moderate confidence:** Homeowners renovating an older house who want individual furniture and materials with character, and are willing to work through maker-led selections. Rosedale's custom pieces and Lafayette's bungalow and vintage approach support this persona. International capability is relevant to scope, but no client nationality, income, or budget is assumed. [Rosedale][rose], [Lafayette Avenue][laf], [About][about].

| Visual usefulness | **Clear for the inspected Rosedale kitchen view; partial as a specification aid.** Room imagery is associated with named projects, without identifying every product visually. [Rosedale][rose] |
| Client clarity | **Partial.** Offer and contact route are present; local project engagement steps are less explicit in the reviewed narrative. [About][about], [Contact][contact] |

Useful to clients: material and craft choices have names. Missing from this sample: a consistent explanation of prior use, finish specification, and care. The 37-day public-feed interval alone is not evidence of weak service.

## 10. Weakness or gap
**Evidence gap, moderate confidence:** Rosedale and Lafayette establish reuse, but the public descriptions do not give consistent previous-use records, quantities, or care instructions. This limits comparison for a client choosing specifically on provenance. The dead-stock canvas post shows that recent material storytelling exists, so Casa Verde should not claim this territory is empty. A credible response, if supported by actual records, would connect each reclaimed selection to its source, finish, repair options, and trade-offs. [Rosedale][rose], [Lafayette Avenue][laf], [canvas post](https://www.instagram.com/stellyselway/p/DbnTME4E6Cl/).
