# J.Fisher Interiors

Research date and access date for every source: **2026-09-16**. Phase 2b. Inclusion: **provisional fit; comparable budget unverified**.

Evidence was inspected in the connected live browser using Playwright and accessibility views. Status labels distinguish observed public content, firm claims, research inferences, and unknowns. Except for the dates stated below, source publication dates are unknown. Raw screenshots and snapshots preserve original wording; authored text uses faithful paraphrases.

## 1. Positioning sentence
**Firm claim, paraphrase of firm positioning:** An Austin full-service interior design practice creating personal homes through craftsmanship, art, and close designer involvement. The Instagram bio is consistent with that emphasis; sustainability appears in service detail rather than the lead promise. [Homepage][home], [About][about], [Architectural interiors][services], [Instagram][ig].

## 2. Project types served
**Firm claim:** Residential design, architectural interiors, furnishing and styling, alongside commercial work. The About page states that Jennifer leads each project. **Observed sample:** Hyde Park, Judges Hill, Mid-Atlantic, Woodview, and Quail Park. [About][about], [Portfolio][portfolio].

Hyde Park is a 2,000-square-foot condo renovation with a kitchen, 2.5 bathrooms, and dining-area expansion. Judges Hill is a 5,500-square-foot mid-century residence with selective remodeling and reuse of existing pieces. Mid-Atlantic is a 6,000-square-foot furnishing-led residence. Woodview describes furnishing an Austin spec home over two years. Quail Park is the designer's own 1971 home and explicitly includes work still pending; it is not counted as a fully completed client commission. [Hyde Park][hyde], [Judges Hill][judge], [Mid-Atlantic][mid], [Woodview][wood], [Quail Park][quail].

Completion dates are **unknown** in these narratives. The gallery does not establish a reliable latest-five order. The sample is four client cases plus one explicitly identified self-project.

## 3. Signature materials
**Firm claim, recurring:** White oak cabinetry and soapstone occur in both Hyde Park and Quail Park. Hyde Park additionally names Luce de Luna stone, Cristallo del Mar, terracotta, zellige, and marble. These named materials are not proof of reclaimed status or low emissions. [Hyde Park][hyde], [Quail Park][quail].

Judges Hill says existing pieces were reused and reworked, but does not identify quantities or previous origins. Woodview describes custom furnishing and lighting without sufficient wood-species detail. Native planting, finish certifications, and a material-care schedule remain **unknown**. Next check: project-specific supplier and finish records. [Judges Hill][judge], [Woodview][wood].

## 4. Pricing range
**unknown, not disclosed:** Total project band, hourly rate, retainer, design fees, procurement charges, and minimum engagement were not found on the reviewed service and project pages. The design guide requires name and email; its contents were not accessed and no form was submitted. [Architectural interiors][services], [Guide landing page][guide].

Mid-Atlantic describes a client without budget restrictions, but gives no numerical cost. It cannot establish a price range. Smallest furnishing-led engagement in this sample: Woodview, with area unknown. Largest published area: Mid-Atlantic at 6,000 square feet; the 5,500-square-foot Judges Hill project has more clearly described renovation work. Area and scope do not establish relative costs. Budget fit remains **unverified**. Next check: public fee guidance with a defined basis. [Mid-Atlantic][mid], [Woodview][wood], [Judges Hill][judge].

## 5. Geographic focus in Austin
**Firm claim:** Austin service is explicit. Hyde Park and Judges Hill are named neighborhood cases; Woodview is explicitly described as an Austin home. Quail Park's reviewed story establishes the designer's self-project but does not supply an equally clear location statement; Mid-Atlantic is also unresolved geographically. Conservative sample count: three Austin-associated cases and two locations **unknown**. Exact addresses and client demographics are not inferred. [About][about], [Hyde Park][hyde], [Judges Hill][judge], [Woodview][wood], [Quail Park][quail], [Mid-Atlantic][mid].

## 7. Ideal client persona
**Inference, moderate confidence:** Homeowners who want a designer closely involved in coordinating a personal, color- and art-led renovation while retaining selected existing pieces. The stated founder involvement and Judges Hill's collaborative reuse support this reading. No occupation, income, or age is assumed. [About][about], [Judges Hill][judge].

## 10. Weakness or gap
**Evidence gap, moderate confidence:** The general low-VOC and reclaimed-wood promise is not connected to named finish specifications in the sampled projects. Judges Hill's documented furniture reuse is meaningful counterevidence against treating sustainability as wholly unsupported. For a client concerned about material health, the remaining gap is specification-level proof. Casa Verde could respond with product names, applicable standards, and maintenance information if those records exist, without promising health outcomes. [Architectural interiors][services], [Judges Hill][judge], [Hyde Park][hyde], [Quail Park][quail].
